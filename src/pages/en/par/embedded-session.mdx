# Embedded session

You can display PAR **inside an `iframe`** on your site, so the cardholder enrolls their autopays without leaving your page. The integration is the same as in a full window: only where the login form opens changes.

## Embed PAR {{ id: 'embed-par' }}

To display PAR in an `iframe`, submit your channel's login form —the one for [merchants](/en/par/merchants/enrollment#login) or for [issuers](/en/par/issuers/login)— with its `target` attribute pointing to the `iframe`.

## Authorized origins {{ id: 'allowed-origins' }}

PAR can only be embedded from the **origins authorized** for your partner. An origin is the scheme, the domain and, if any, the port of your site, for example `https://www.example.com`.

Ask your Placetopay contact to register every origin you will embed PAR from, in each environment. PAR also uses them to validate the issuers' return and error URLs.

## Session lifecycle {{ id: 'session-lifecycle' }}

The cardholder's session does not depend on cookies, so it works inside an `iframe` from another domain even if the browser blocks third-party cookies. In exchange, it lives only in the PAR page that is open, and that has two consequences for your integration:

- **If the `iframe` is reloaded or its URL is loaded again, the session ends.** To start over, request a new token and submit the form again.
- **When the session ends**, because of its maximum duration or because it was closed, PAR shows the cardholder that the session ended, inside the same `iframe`.

When the enrollment finishes, PAR sends the cardholder back to your return URL in the **main window**, not inside the `iframe`, so your site takes back control of the full page.
