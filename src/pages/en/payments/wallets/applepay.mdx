Apple Pay is Apple’s digital wallet, which lets users securely store their payment methods on their devices and use them to pay online without entering card details. In Webcheckout, Apple manages the payment authorization while Placetopay receives and processes the transaction. {{ className: 'lead' }}

## Benefits of Apple Pay

- **One-tap payment**: The customer picks a card and confirms with Face ID, Touch ID or their passcode, without typing any data.
- **Enhanced security**: Apple never shares the real card number; the transaction is processed with a network token and its cryptogram.
- **Buyer confidence**: Authorization happens through the device’s own security mechanisms.
- **Prefilled payer data**: Apple Pay can provide the name, email and phone number verified in the customer’s Apple ID.
- **Works alongside other wallets**: It can be offered in the same flow together with Click to Pay, Google Pay and the Placetopay wallet.

## Availability

- **Devices and browsers**: The Apple Pay button is only displayed when the customer’s device and browser support the wallet. It is available in Safari on iOS and macOS, and in desktop Chrome and Edge on macOS, where the customer authorizes the payment by scanning a code with their iPhone.
- **Processing**: Requires a processing network enabled for network token payments.
- **Supported features**: 3D Secure, credits and interest calculation.

## Apple Pay in embedded integrations {{ id: 'embedded-integrations' }}

If your site embeds Webcheckout inside an iframe with your own solution, keep the following in mind:

- **Safari and iOS browsers**: Because of these browsers’ limitations with embedded content, do not keep Webcheckout inside the iframe. Open it through a redirect or a popup window so Apple Pay works.
- **Other browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox)**: The iframe must include the `allow="payment"` attribute to allow Apple Pay from the embedded context.

<Note>
These scenarios are already handled by Placetopay’s official library: the [Lightbox SDK](/en/checkout/sdk/lightbox) redirects or opens a popup window on Safari and iOS, and adds the `allow="payment"` permission on other browsers. If you plan to display Apple Pay, we recommend using it instead of your own iframe.
</Note>

## Use cases

Apple Pay is ideal for:
- **Customers on Apple devices**: iPhone, iPad and Mac users who already have their cards in Wallet.
- **Fast mobile purchases**: Flows where reducing manual data entry increases conversion.
- **Merchants seeking tokenization**: Transactions processed with a network token and cryptogram instead of the card number.
- **Merchants with multiple wallets**: Complements Click to Pay, Google Pay and the Placetopay wallet in the same payment flow.

## Advantages of using Apple Pay

- **Lower cart abandonment**: Payments are confirmed in seconds with no card forms.
- **Customer trust**: Apple’s experience and reputation add perceived security for buyers.
- **Less exposure of sensitive data**: The merchant never receives the real card number.
- **Dedicated technical support**: Placetopay supports the enablement and follow-up of the integration.

## FAQs

<details>
<summary>How do I enable Apple Pay for my business?</summary>
<p>Contact Placetopay support to enable the wallet on your site. Apple Pay is available in Webcheckout and in flows such as microsites and payment links.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What is Apple Pay?</summary>
<p>Apple Pay is Apple’s digital wallet. It lets users store cards on their device and use them to pay online by confirming with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>On which devices and browsers can my customer pay?</summary>
<p>On Safari for iOS and macOS, and on desktop Chrome and Edge on macOS, where authorization is completed by scanning a code with the iPhone. The button is only displayed if the device has the wallet available.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if the customer does not have Apple Pay available?</summary>
<p>The button is not displayed and the customer can complete the payment with the other methods enabled in Webcheckout.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can Apple Pay be combined with 3D Secure?</summary>
<p>Yes. Apple Pay supports 3D Secure within Placetopay’s processing flow.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I offer installment payments with Apple Pay?</summary>
<p>Yes, when processing allows it. Installment selection does not happen in the Apple Pay interface but in the Webcheckout flow.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I test Apple Pay before going live?</summary>
<p>Apple does not expose an environment parameter: test mode is enabled on the device. You must sign in with a Sandbox Tester Apple account and add one of Apple’s published test cards to Wallet. Real cards do not work in testing and test cards do not work in production.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Apple Pay replace the other wallets?</summary>
<p>No. Apple Pay is not exclusive and can coexist in the same flow with Click to Pay, Google Pay and the Placetopay wallet.</p>
</details>
